    Fraud awareness tips

    Common patterns behind UPI scams — worth knowing even before you run a check.

    You never pay to receive money

    Refunds, cashback, prizes, and lottery winnings are credited to you. Any request to pay first "to unlock" a refund or prize is a scam.

    Screen sharing apps are a red flag

    No legitimate bank, UPI app, or support agent will ever ask you to install a remote screen-sharing app during a call.

    Collect requests can drain your account

    Approving a "collect request" sends money out of your account — it does not bring money in. Read the amount and payee carefully before approving anything.

    Urgency is a manipulation tactic

    Scammers create time pressure ("offer expires in 5 minutes") to stop you from verifying. A legitimate transaction can wait a few minutes.

    About PayProtect

    PayProtect is a frontend prototype that helps you sanity-check a payment before you send it. It runs a set of rule-based checks against the UPI ID, payee name, amount, and stated reason for payment, entirely inside your browser — there's no backend, no live fraud database, and no data ever leaves your device.

    It's built as an awareness tool, not a certified fraud-detection system. Always verify a payee through your bank or UPI app before making a real payment.
